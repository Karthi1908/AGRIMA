# Problem Definition: Structured Product Payoff Visualization & Scenario Comprehension

## Problem Statement

Structured product sales specialists and wealth managers struggle to communicate non-linear payoff mechanics (such as knock-in, knock-out, observation barriers, and capital-at-risk triggers) to clients because static termsheets and spreadsheet tables fail to demonstrate multi-scenario outcomes dynamically. As a result, investors face substantial cognitive friction understanding how market movements impact their capital and returns, leading to delayed investment decisions, misaligned risk expectations, and lost sales opportunities.

## Affected Users & Stakeholders

- **Users**:
  - **Structured Products Sales Specialists & Relationship Managers**: Face difficulty illustrating multi-contingent barrier outcomes (knock-in/knock-out) to clients during time-constrained advisory sessions due to rigid, static presentation tools.
  - **Private Wealth & Retail Investors (Clients)**: Struggle to decipher dense contractual termsheets, lacking intuitive means to grasp conditional payoff behavior across varying market paths and stress scenarios.

  - **Derivatives Structuring Desks**: [NEEDS CLARIFICATION: Desks producing the product termsheets who require distribution teams to represent payoff mechanics accurately without misinterpretation.]

## Goals

- Provide sales specialists with clear, dynamic means to explain complex non-linear payoff structures and barrier conditions (knock-in / knock-out) in real time.
- Enable clients to intuitively grasp product payoff behaviors, barrier buffers, and downside tail-risk conditions across hypothetical market paths before committing capital.
- Drastically reduce client cognitive load and friction when reviewing legal and commercial termsheet parameters.
- Standardize and accelerate the process of turning raw termsheets into accessible, client-ready scenario visualizations.

## Non-Goals

- Generating regulated financial, investment, or legal advice or automated product recommendations.
- Trade execution, order routing, trade booking, or direct lifecycle settlement integration.
- Live quantitative option pricing, dynamic Greeks hedging, or real-time secondary market-making calculation engines.
- General-purpose enterprise contract repository or legal document management system.

If unaddressed, sales desks will continue relying on fragile, static spreadsheets and lengthy text documents that obscure contingent risks.
